How Our Tool Analyzes Your Body Condition ?

Your daily calorie needs are calculated based on the info you provide during sign-up, including your age, gender, height, and weight. We then compare your total daily calorie intake with your estimated calorie requirement and show your body condition in 3 levels: Low, Normal, High which you can read more about it down below.

1. Level: Low

Low means your calorie intake is below your body's daily needs. You may not be consuming enough energy to support your body.

  • 1/3
  • Low

2. Level: Normal

Normal means your calorie intake matches your estimated daily requirement, indicating a balanced intake.

  • 2/3
  • Normal

3. Level: High

High means your calorie intake exceeds your estimated daily requirement. Consuming excess calories over time may lead to unwanted weight gain and other health issues.

  • 3/3
  • High
